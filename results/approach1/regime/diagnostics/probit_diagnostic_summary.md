## 6. Factual assessment for the group (no final decision)

- **Optimizer audit:** the primary estimates are numerically trustworthy in this sample: all 82 Newton fits converged, with no warnings, no non-zero warnflag and no ridge fallback. The original wrapper's hard-coded convergence flag is nevertheless a code-quality weakness that should be corrected in any future refactor.
- **Inner-CV rank sensitivity:** the inner criterion is leakage-safe within each outer fold and produced no fallback folds. It selected r=1 in 76/82 folds and improved mean inner Brier from about 0.243 to 0.212. On the 82 OOS months the Brier/AUC differences versus the primary are small and descriptive; this experiment does not beat the prior-frequency Brier or the persistence AUC. It provides credible evidence that a lower-dimensional Probit is more stable, but not by itself sufficient evidence to replace the primary specification.
- **Dynamic Probit sensitivity:** it converged in all folds but produced larger coefficients, worse conditioning and worse OOS Brier/AUC than the primary. The evidence does not support the dynamic extension as an improvement.
- **Decision status:** no model is selected or promoted here. The primary remains the GDP-nowcast-selected specification; any change requires the human team's explicit approval.
