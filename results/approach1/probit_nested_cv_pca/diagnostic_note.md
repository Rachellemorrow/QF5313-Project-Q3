# Strictly nested inner-CV sensitivity - diagnostic note

This note is generated by `code/approach1_probit_nested_cv_pca.py`. It is a sensitivity experiment, not `report.md`, and it makes no final model-selection decision.

## Design

The target, outer forecast origins, outer training windows, predictor universe, release-lag treatment and 82 evaluation observations are identical to the existing Probit experiment. For each outer fold, inner expanding-window validation uses the same k=60..n-1 split as the previous diagnostic. The difference is that the indicator screen, scaler, missing-value fill, PCA loadings and LPR standardization are refitted from scratch inside every inner fold, using only that inner fold's training predictor rows. The inner validation predictor is transformed with those fitted parameters and never participates in fitting. The validation label is used only to compute Brier.

After the rank is selected, the screen, scaler, PCA and Probit are refitted on the outer training predictor rows only; the outer test predictor is transformed with that outer-training preprocessing. The outer test target is used only for evaluation.

- Mean inner Brier at the selected rank: 0.2489; at the primary rank: 0.2882.
- Outer OOS: Brier 0.2311, AUC 0.5879, accuracy 0.6341, balanced accuracy 0.5293, n=82, positives=27.

Calibration in the large: nested 0.0251 vs primary 0.0385. Mean predicted probability: nested 0.3543 vs observed 0.3293. Brier reliability/resolution: nested 0.0346/0.0265; primary 0.0833/0.0497.

The nested model's Brier is slightly lower than the primary and the prior-frequency baseline, but its AUC is lower and its balanced accuracy is lower. The improvement is therefore mainly in calibration/accuracy at the 0.5 threshold, not in ranking. This distinction matters: the nested model does not dominate the primary or the baselines.

## Interpretation

- The new procedure is genuinely nested: every inner fold has its own screen, scaler and PCA fitted only on its training predictor rows; the inner validation row and the outer test row are never in those fitting samples.
- The candidate set is the same 1..8 set. Infeasible ranks, if any, are recorded in `outer_rank_selection.csv`; no candidate is silently dropped.
- OOS differences are descriptive, not a selection criterion. The rank was selected only by mean inner-validation Brier.

## Limitations

- The nested outer model fits screen/scaler/PCA on the outer training-label predictor rows, whereas the primary fits PCA on all monthly rows up to the origin. This is a deliberate nested design; it is a comparison caveat, not a claim that the primary is wrong.
- The training sample is small (74-155 outer labels), so rank selection and coefficients have limited precision.
- The 49.5 threshold is not mixed into this experiment.
- Validation: 27/27 checks passed; all passed: True.
